## Opportunities
1. Make homepage an accessible entry point into real beginner, workbench and original lore content.
2. Eliminate navigation confusion and desktop overflow on mobile; validate native WP navigation on real devices.
3. Make tutorials more useful with original photographs, time/tools/skill level, materials, mistakes, costs and concise FAQ.
4. Build internal topic clusters around generic miniature tools, airbrush maintenance, paint application and terrain.
5. Use real Search Console query/page data before deciding what content to produce or prune.
6. Add real creator/byline, testing methodology and affiliate disclosure where appropriate.
7. Measure click paths: homepage → category → article → relevant external equipment, only after legal/IP and affiliate approval.

## Business/legal constraint
GW fan content can carry commercial/IP restrictions. Their legal guidance must be checked in its current form before linking revenue to dependent content. A prominent 'unofficial' label is useful but not a license. Keep original Warpstorm lore and generic hobby commerce separate from fan-fiction/franchise materials.
