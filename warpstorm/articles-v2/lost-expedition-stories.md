# 7 Lost Expeditions Into the Warpstorm

**Short answer:** Seven fleets entered the Warpstorm and never returned as themselves: a colonisation convoy that aged backwards, a salvage armada found rusted shut, a pilgrim fleet whose only transmission now loops forever at the storm's edge. These are short, atmospheric stories from the dark side of our original sci-fi universe.

*URL: /lore/lost-expedition-stories/ · Primary keyword: dark sci-fi short stories*

> *Warpstorm is an original fictional universe. All expeditions below are fiction. Not affiliated with any other sci-fi franchise.*

## 1. The Provision Convoy *Halcyon Grace*

Eleven colony ships, escorted, fully provisioned, left the Meridian Yards for the outer rim. They entered the storm corridor on schedule. Nineteen hours later, the *Halcyon Grace* emerged — alone, intact, and eleven years older than its departure date. Its crew of 4,000 had become 612. The survivors remembered a crossing that "took a while." The colony fleet behind them was never found.

## 2. The Salvage Armada *Iron Tithe*

Eight salvage vessels answered a ghost-ship distress call inside the storm. A patrol found the armada four months later, all eight hulls fused nose-to-stern in a single chain, every airlock welded from the *inside*. No bodies. The salvage in their holds was logged as complete — cargo that no registry has ever matched.

## 3. The Pilgrim Fleet *Voice of the Deep*

Three thousand faithful follow a signal they believe comes from beyond the storm's heart. Every year they sail one wave closer. Every year their transmission repeats, unchanged, on the same frequency: *"We are almost there. We are almost there. We are almost there."* The signal predates the fleet by two centuries.

## 4. The Charting Mission *Cartographer's Oath*

The Oath's mission was to map the storm's interior for the first time in a generation. Its final log entry — verified authentic — reads: *"Chart complete. Do not use. Do not use. The routes are alive. Do not—"*

The chart was transmitted, in full, to fleet command. It is accurate. It is complete. Every navigator who has followed it has been lost.

## 5. The Dredger *Marrow Sun*

A mining dredger harvesting storm-metal from a wreck field reported impossible yields — ore that continued humming after extraction. On the final day of contact, its captain transmitted a single image: the dredger's own hull, photographed from the outside, rusted beyond recognition, *while the crew was still alive inside it*.

## 6. The Escort Squadron *Nine Lanterns*

Nine warships escorted a courier through a surge. Eight emerged. The ninth — the *Lantern Vigil* — continues to appear on every subsequent expedition's sensors, always ahead of the fleet, always escorting, never answering hails. Veterans refuse to acknowledge it. It has been seen for forty years.

## 7. The Returned *Second Dawn*

The only expedition to return *changed but alive*. The crew stepped out of the airlock, reported an uneventful crossing, and requested their pay. They had been gone eighty years. They had not aged. They all — every member, independently — request the same posting: back into the storm. Fleet command has denied all of them. Twice.
