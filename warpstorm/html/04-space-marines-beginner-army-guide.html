Space Marines Beginner Army Guide: Your First 1,000 Points (2026)

Short answer: Space Marines are the best first army in Warhammer 40K: tough, forgiving, cheap to start and supported by more tutorials than any other faction. Build your first 1,000 points around a solid core of Battleline Intercessors, one assault unit, one vehicle and a Captain. Everything in the starter boxes counts — nothing is wasted.

Why Space Marines Are the Beginner Army

Every edition, every starter box and half of all hobby content on the internet is built around Space Marines. For a beginner, that translates into three real advantages:

  1. Tough on the table. Good armour saves and decent toughness mean your mistakes don't delete your army in one enemy phase.
  2. Cheap and available. Starter sets, Combat Patrol boxes and second-hand markets are flooded with Marines — the easiest army to buy and sell.
  3. Infinite learning material. Whatever question you have — painting, list-building, tactics — someone has answered it with photos and videos.

Paint Scheme: Simple and Sharp

The classic mistake is choosing a scheme with ten steps. Pick a one-colour armour scheme:

  • Basecoat the whole model in your chapter colour (two thin coats)
  • Wash with a black or brown shade — instant depth
  • Drybrush or edge-highlight with a lighter tone
  • Details last: gun metal, eye lenses (a dot of colour), gold on the shoulder trim

Two evenings of painting gets ten models done. Save the freehand banners and glow effects for squad number three, not squad number one.

Playing Your First Games

  • Keep the army in a castle formation early: Marines are durable enough to hold ground and shoot — the simplest way to learn objectives.
  • Charge only when you're sure you'll win. Learning melee math the hard way is expensive.
  • Score objectives, not kills. Beginners lose games by chasing kills; the mission rewards standing on circles.

Common Marine Beginner Mistakes

  • Spreading units across the whole board — Marines win by concentrating fire and holding midfield
  • Leaving the Captain home — leader buffs make your Battleline units dramatically better
  • Painting checkered chapter schemes too early — chunky colour blocks first, detail later

FAQ — Space Marines for Beginners

Are Space Marines good for beginners?

Yes — the best choice. They're forgiving in-game, easy to build and paint, and every starter product includes them. You'll never struggle to find advice, opponents or spare models.

How much does a 1,000-point Space Marine army cost?

Roughly $250–400 including tools and paints, less if you start from a starter box or buy second-hand. The starter boxes alone cover the first 500 points.

Which Space Marine chapter should I paint?

One with a simple scheme: a solid blue (Ultramarines-style), green or red. Save complex chapter schemes for later armies. Homebrew chapters are fully legitimate — invent your own colours.

Are Space Marines still strong in the current edition?

Marines are always a mid-to-strong, well-supported faction — Games Workshop balances around them first. As a beginner, their forgiving durability matters far more than meta tier lists.

What do I buy after the starter box?

One box of Intercessors and one heavy/vehicle box gets you to a real 1,000 points. Buy, build, paint, repeat — one box at a time.

Space Marines vs. Necrons for a first army?

Both are top beginner picks. Marines win on community support and starter-box availability; Necrons win on looks and fastest paint scheme. Read our head-to-head comparison.
