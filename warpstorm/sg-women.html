The Five-Piece Rule

1. A corset or corset belt — the anchor of the outfit

2. One styled layer — tee, hoodie or tailcoat

3. Goggles — on your hat, around your neck or pushed up on your head

4. One brass accessory — pocket watch, ring or coat buttons

5. Shoes you can actually walk in — boots beat heels at cons

FAQ

How much does a full steampunk outfit for women cost?

Between $72 (casual) and $155 (full Victorian look) at real catalog prices. The average complete look lands around $110.

Can I wear steampunk every day (not just cons)?

Yes — look #4 (tee + corset belt + jeans) is designed exactly for that. Goggles on a bag strap keep the vibe without full costume.

What goes first: corset or goggles?

The corset. It defines the silhouette; goggles are the finishing accent. Start with a $44.95 corset top and build around it.

Do steampunk outfits work for plus-size women?

Yes — lace-up corsets are adjustable by design, and the hoodie/tee looks scale to any size. Start with look #6 or #11.

How do I make the outfit look "real" and not costume-y?

Limit yourself to three steampunk elements and keep the rest in muted colours (brown, black, olive, brass). One brass accessory reads as "style", five read as costume.
